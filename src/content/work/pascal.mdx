## Design around the note

Pascal keeps the editor in focus while a writer switches between a draft and a reference.

## Design process

1. Keep the active note visible in both the sidebar and its tab.
2. Group formatting actions by style, block, and media.
3. Give the command palette one field with separate groups for Notes and Actions.
